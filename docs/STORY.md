# HOTSHOT CALIFORNIA — story bible (spoilers)

## The spine
In 1987 a producer named **Aurelio Vance** shot an action movie in the Mojave called **HOTSHOT**. For the climax he wanted one real take: a car on fire, with a stunt performer who wasn't told the fire extinguishers were empty. Stunt driver **Tommy Moreno** died on camera. The film was never released. The footage was.

Vance's company now makes **"HOTSHOT CALIFORNIA"**, a late-night true-crime TV show hosted by **Marv Kessel**. The show's ratings depend on exclusive footage of massacres. Vance manufactures those massacres. He finds grieving, violent or desperate people, mails them a tape, a Polaroid and a *Persona*, and points them at somebody who knows too much about 1987. Crew members hidden in each location (and security cameras) film everything.

## What "Hotshot" means (in the order the player learns it)
1. **A cocky showoff.** That's how the Voice calls Cass on the phone.
2. **The film.** HOTSHOT (1987), unreleased.
3. **The pyrotechnic "hot shot."** The final, live-fire take, which killed Tommy.
4. **A hotshot of heroin.** A lethal dose. It's how Vance disposes of people who stop being useful (Bobby's thread, 1992).
5. **Hotshot crews.** Wildfire firefighters. Wes Coyle was one. In the 1992 finale the hills are burning.
6. **The shot itself.** Everyone is "the lead" of Vance's picture. The final reveal: the player has been watching the tapes all along. The mission structure *is* the edit. The fake menus and corrupted saves are the moments where the edit shows through.

## Timeline
- **1987**: Tommy dies on the HOTSHOT set. Crew members: Harcourt (special effects safety), Pruitt's father (location security), the Director (Vance's alias).
- **1988**: Cass (THE STAR) goes to the Sunset Palms. It's the slice's Chapter I.
- **1990**: Tilly (THE FOOL), Luz (THE GHOST) and Deacon (THE SAINT) each get tapes. The Galaxy Palace arcade, a Beverly Hills burglary, and the "Blue Hour" nightclub all turn out to be the same night seen from three angles.
- **1991**: Officer Dana Pruitt investigates the Star Killer and becomes the next Star. Nadia Vasquez (THE ANGEL) administers "hotshots" for Vance at Cedars and wants out. Marv Kessel (THE KING) starts believing he's the one directing.
- **1992**: Wildfire season. Wes (THE COWBOY) and Bobby (THE DOG) converge on Vance's desert compound, which is the original HOTSHOT set, rebuilt. Cass returns. The final mission is a live broadcast.

## Choices that matter in the slice
- Calling the police or not (small callback in 1991, from Pruitt).
- **Sparing or executing Harcourt.** If spared, Harcourt returns in 1991 as an informant who gives Dana the first real lead on Vance. If executed, the lead comes from the Polaroid instead, and in the finale only one character survives.
- **Staying on the line** to hear "Tommy" (flag `heard_tommy_1988`). This opens the alternate ending, in which Tommy is alive and was the one who sold the footage.
- Killing Earl (flag `killed_civilian_earl`) changes the news report and removes Earl from 1991.
