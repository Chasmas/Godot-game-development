# Window reconstruction preview

Source: reviewed ImageGen `window_reference.png`. Native Blender geometry separates the two glass panes, frame, sill, blind slats, pleated curtains and brass rod. Painted frame colour uses the reviewed ImageGen finish. Curtain folds are authored geometry. The source sunset is not baked into the glass, allowing the environment to supply reflections.

Initial render completed successfully in Blender 5.2.2 and was inspected. Frame overlap at centre rail joints was found and removed in the builder; rerender required to verify that correction. The curtain fabric is currently smooth and needs reference-quality weave and edge stitching. Blind slats are too thin relative to the reference. Finials, brackets and blind cords remain missing. Animation, damage state, gameplay projection, scale and collision remain unvalidated. Not integrated or production approved.

Second pass completed successfully and the new render was inspected. The upper and lower centre-frame dark overlap bands are gone. Brass finials, wall brackets, rod supports, four blind ladder cords and two pull cords with pulls are present as separate geometry. Blind slat depth increased from 0.045 to 0.078 m, but their near-horizontal angle still leaves narrow visible strips in this camera; further angle/profile adjustment is needed to match the reference. Fabric weave, hem stitching, animation, damage, gameplay projection and collision remain outstanding. This is still a reconstruction preview.

Third pass: slat angle changed to -65 degrees to expose the broad faces. Both curtains now contain a 49-frame, 24 fps gentle-draft shape-key animation with 22 mm lower-edge depth displacement and 9 mm sideways displacement. An initial anchor assertion detected slight top-row movement caused by fold height variation; the deformation now explicitly fixes every top-row vertex. The rebuilt blend completed with exit code 0, and assertions verify stationary top rows and lower cloth displacement greater than 20 mm. This validates the authored deformation, not Godot playback. Fabric detail, damage and gameplay integration are still pending.

GLB animation validation: the builder exported `window_review.glb` with morph targets and animations. `tools/m01_window_animation_review.tscn` loaded it directly through Godot GLTFDocument (no editor-import assumptions), generated a scene and verified exactly two morph-enabled curtains. AnimationPlayer seeking from the beginning to the midpoint changes both curtain shape weights by more than 0.5. Godot 4.7.2 completed with exit code 0 and `M01_WINDOW_ANIMATION`. This confirms animation data transfer and engine playback evaluation; it does not prove visual rendering, 2D prerender playback, collision or integration into the actual map.

GPU viewport review: a real compatibility-renderer capture is saved to `build/m01_window_godot_review.png`. The first capture showed opaque-looking glass and excessive review lighting. The isolated review now overrides glass with explicit thin alpha material (0.22) and reduces light energy. This compatibility fallback is confined to the review scene; the source GLB is unchanged. Rendering completes successfully with the animation checks retained. The model is visible in the engine, but this standalone view does not validate placement or appearance in M01. Renderer-specific glass behavior and material matching remain integration requirements.
