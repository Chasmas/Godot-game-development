Blender 5.2.2 completed `tools/art/review_m01_guest_door.py` with exit code 0. The saved blend packs the reviewed walnut and painted finish sources. The render was visually inspected.

The slab, mouldings, plaque, peephole, kickplate and handle are parented to a hinge at the left edge. The frame remains independent. No gameplay geometry or level data changed.

Second pass: removed the overlapping jamb/lintel faces responsible for the dark upper bands, added a brass key cylinder, key slot and lever rose. Both closed and 85-degree open renders were inspected. Blender assertions verify that the slab centre moves more than 0.4 m while all three stationary frame parts remain unchanged; results are recorded in `hinge_review.json`. Remaining: clearer peephole inset; closer reference matching and richer wear; gameplay cutaway projection, readable in-game scale and collision validation. The Blender hinge check does not prove Godot interaction behavior. This is a reconstruction preview, not a production-approved asset.

## Gameplay projection candidate

The current Godot `Door` uses a leaf along local +x from its hinge, independently rotated physics and a 3 px thick rectangular collider. A top-down orthographic leaf-only PNG has now been rendered and inspected. The stationary frame is excluded so it cannot rotate with the door. `leaf_projection_contract.json` records the measured camera-space hinge, tip and scale for a 32 px leaf. At that scale the 0.055 m slab occupies about 2.05 px; decorative hardware extends beyond it and must be assessed for misleading collision readability. No runtime replacement has been enabled. A damaged counterpart, illumination and actual in-game swing review remain necessary.

The isolated Godot scene `tools/m01_door_projection_review.tscn` completed successfully in Godot 4.7.2 using compatibility rendering. It subclasses the production Door solely to override drawing. At 0, 45 and 85 degrees, assertions verified collider rotation and the production `hit_point` endpoint against the 32 px leaf length. The real viewport capture `build/m01_door_projection_review.png` was inspected: the leaf remains anchored at the cyan review hinge at all three angles. The view is magnified five times, so it does not establish normal map readability. This check does not cover walking, kicking, bullet blocking, wall stops, breakage or map illumination; the production Door drawing remains unchanged.
