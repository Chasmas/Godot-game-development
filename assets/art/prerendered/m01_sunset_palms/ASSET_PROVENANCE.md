# M01 asset provenance

## Environment authoring
- Exterior composition, architecture, pool, parking, lighting, vegetation and layer renders: Blender scene `sunset_palms_courtyard.blend`, generated by `tools/art/build_prerendered_motel.py`.
- Interior rooms, service transition, reception and connective corridor: Blender staging scene generated by `tools/art/build_m01_interiors.py`.
- Pixel preview derivatives are generated by `tools/art/pixelize_m01_staging.py` and remain staging-only.

## Meshy sources
- Meshy is a source for individual 3D characters and props (`assets/art/Artwork/3d/**`), fetched/processed by `tools/art/meshy3d.py` and assembled/baked by Blender tools.
- Meshy assets are not the environment layout. They must be validated for silhouette, orientation, scale and gameplay readability before promotion.

## Integration rule
Godot keeps the gameplay layout, collisions, objectives, navigation and camera contract. Blender replaces visual dressing only after visual QA and gameplay smoke pass. Never treat a raw Meshy model or a Blender blockout as a final level environment.
